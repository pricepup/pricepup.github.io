PricePup privacy policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

PricePup (pricepup.github.io) is a free grocery price app for Calgary, Alberta. You can scan a barcode, log a price, and see where a product is cheapest. PricePup is made by one person, has no ads, and keeps as little data as it can.

Questions or requests: pricepup.app@gmail.com

Google sign-in and Google user data

You sign in with Google to see the shared prices and to add your own. When you do, PricePup receives only:

  • your name, and
  • your email address.

We use them only to keep your PricePup account (your saved items, coins and settings) the same on every phone you sign in on, to show your first name to friends you connect with, and to reply if you contact us. We do not read your Gmail, contacts, calendar, Drive files or anything else in your Google account. We do not sell, share or transfer Google user data to anyone, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not use it to train AI models.

What we save

  • Your Google name and email, as above.
  • Prices and products you add, including small product photos you take. These are shared with everyone, because that is how PricePup works. Other users see the price, the store and the date, not your name.
  • Your in-app progress: coins, favourites and settings, and your friends list, if you use it.
  • Messages you send through "Tell us how we can improve".

What we never save

  • Your location. PricePup uses your phone's GPS only on your phone, at that moment, to check you are in Calgary and to suggest the nearest store. Your location is never sent to us or stored.
  • Your IP address, your contacts, or anything else on your phone.

Who can see what

  • Prices and products are public to all PricePup users, without your name.
  • Friends you connect with see your first name.
  • We do not sell data and we do not show ads.

Services we use

  • Google Firebase: sign-in and the database where your account data is stored.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the app files.
  • Open Food Facts: looks up product names by barcode.
  • Open-Meteo: Calgary weather shown in the app.
  • Ko-fi: only if you choose to support PricePup. Payments are handled by Ko-fi, not by us.

Delete your data

Email pricepup.app@gmail.com or send a message with "Tell us how we can improve" in the app's Settings, and we will delete your account data (name, email, progress, friends and messages). Prices you shared stay in the database, without your name.

You can also remove PricePup's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
