# PricePup

A free grocery price app for Calgary. No ads.

**Open the app: https://pricepup.github.io**

- Scan a barcode or search to see a product's price history, the all-time low and this week's deals across 14 Calgary stores.
- Log a price you see in store and help everyone save.
- Adopt a puppy and earn coins for every price you share.

PricePup works in your phone's browser. Tap "Install" (Android) or Share → "Add to Home Screen" (iPhone) to use it like an app.

Ideas or problems? Send them from Settings → "Tell us how we can improve".
Like PricePup? You can support it on [Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/pricepup).
